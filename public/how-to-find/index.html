How to find a Rotaract club in South Asia

Use Club Finder by Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO) to search, filter, locate clubs near you, or explore the map.

Search by city, area, or club name

  1. Open Club Finder.
  2. Type a city, neighbourhood, or club name.
  3. Press Enter to run the search.
  4. Select a club in the list or on the map for details and public contact options.

Use filters

Open Filters to narrow results by country, state or province, city, district, zone, and club type (community or university). Clear filters anytime, or copy a link to share your current view.

Find clubs near you

Tap Near me and allow location access when asked. Club Finder flies to your area once and lists nearby clubs. You can keep searching or panning the map afterward.

Explore the map

Zoom in and pan across South Asia. When you are zoomed in enough, the list shows clubs near the map center so you can browse by place without typing a query.

Frequently asked questions

How do I search for a Rotaract club by city or name?
Type a city, area, or club name, then press Enter. Matching clubs appear in the list and on the map.
How do I find Rotaract clubs near me?
Use Near me and allow location access. Nearby clubs are sorted by distance from your current location.
Can I filter by district, zone, or club type?
Yes. Filters support country, state or province, city, district, zone, and community or university type.
Who provides Club Finder?
Club Finder is provided by Rotaract South Asia MDIO (RSAMDIO).
